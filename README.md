# Project 3: Customer Churn / Sales Trend Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyses e-commerce sales data to identify monthly revenue trends, compare product categories, understand customer spending patterns, and develop actionable business recommendations.

## Live Dashboard

Explore the interactive sales analytics dashboard:

**https://interncircle-project-3-sales-trend-analysis-aawneya.streamlit.app/**
## Objectives
- Analyse monthly revenue and month-over-month growth.
- Identify the highest and lowest revenue months.
- Compare product categories using groupby analysis and pivot tables.
- Identify high-value customers and calculate their contribution to total revenue.
- Develop data-driven business recommendations.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab

## Dataset
A synthetic dataset containing 1,500 fictional e-commerce orders generated using Python and NumPy. The dataset represents transactions from January to December 2025.

It contains order dates, customer IDs, product categories, and order amounts.

## Key Findings
- April recorded the highest monthly revenue: 164,116.94.
- June recorded the lowest monthly revenue: 122,242.94.
- Fashion generated the highest category revenue: 565,121.54.
- The top 10% of customers contributed 19.50% of total revenue.

## Business Recommendations
1. Strengthen the Fashion category through better stock management and targeted promotions.
2. Investigate the reasons behind the revenue decline in June.
3. Introduce a customer-retention initiative to encourage repeat purchases.
